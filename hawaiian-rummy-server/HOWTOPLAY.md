# Hawaiian Rummy - How to Play

## Game Overview

Hawaiian Rummy is a multi-round card game for 2-4 players. The objective is to have the **lowest score** after 10 rounds. Players create melds (sets and runs) according to specific requirements for each round, then try to get rid of all their cards.

## The Deck

The game uses **3 standard decks plus 6 jokers** (162 cards total):
- 3 decks of 52 cards (156 cards)
- 6 jokers

## Card Values & Wildcards

### Scoring Points (when left in hand)
- **Jokers**: 50 points each
- **2s**: 20 points each
- **Aces**: 15 points each
- **10, J, Q, K**: 10 points each
- **3-9**: 5 points each

### Wildcards
**2s and Jokers are wildcards** and can substitute for any card in melds.

## Game Setup

1. **Join the Lobby**: Enter your name and join the game
2. **Wait for Players**: 2-4 players can join
3. **Start Game**: Any player can start when ready (minimum 2 players)
4. **Deal**: Each player receives 9 cards
5. One card is placed face-up in the discard pile to start

## Round Requirements

Each round has specific meld requirements you must meet before laying off or going out:

| Round | Requirement | Max Buys |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | 2 sets of 3 | 3 |
| 2 | 1 set of 3 and a run of 4 | 3 |
| 3 | 2 runs of 4 | 3 |
| 4 | 3 sets of 3 | 3 |
| 5 | 1 set of 3 and a run of 7 | 3 |
| 6 | 2 sets of 3 and a run of 5 | 3 |
| 7 | 3 sets of 4 | 3 |
| 8 | 1 set of 3 and a run of 10 | 3 |
| 9 | 3 sets of 3 and a run of 5 | 3 |
| 10 | 3 runs of 5 | 4 |

## Turn Structure

On your turn, you must:

### 1. DRAW PHASE
Choose ONE option:
- **Draw from deck**: Take the top card from the deck
- **Take discard pile**: Take the top card from the discard pile (if available and no buy was just processed)

### 2. MELD/LAYOFF PHASE
You can do any of these actions (optional):
- **Create Melds**: Form sets or runs to meet round requirements
- **Cancel Melding**: Return melded cards to your hand if you change your mind
- **Layoff Cards**: Add cards to any player's existing melds (only after meeting requirements)

### 3. DISCARD PHASE
- **Discard one card**: Place one card face-up on the discard pile to end your turn
- If you discard your last card and have met requirements, you win the round!

## Melds Explained

### Sets
A **set** is 3 or more cards of the **same rank** (different suits allowed):
- Example: 7♠ 7♥ 7♦
- Example with wildcard: K♣ K♦ 2♠ (the 2 acts as any King)

### Runs
A **run** is 4 or more consecutive cards of the **same suit**:
- Example: 5♥ 6♥ 7♥ 8♥
- Example with wildcard: 3♦ 2♣ 5♦ 6♦ (the 2 fills in for 4♦)
- **Aces can be low (A-2-3-4) or high (J-Q-K-A)**, but runs cannot wrap around (K-A-2 is invalid)

## The Buy Mechanism

When it's **not your turn**, you can "buy" the discard pile card:

### How to Buy
1. Click **"Request Buy"** when the discard pile has a card you want
2. All players between the current player and you must **Pass** to grant you the buy
3. If granted, you receive:
   - The discard pile card
   - One additional card from the deck (penalty)

### Buy Rules
- Maximum buys per round (see table above, usually 3)
- Cannot buy your own discarded card
- Cannot buy during meld/discard phase
- If the current player takes the discard, all buy requests are cancelled
- After a buy is processed, the current player **must** draw from the deck (cannot take discard)

### Buy Priority
The player **closest to the current player** (clockwise) has priority if multiple players request a buy.

## Laying Off Cards

After meeting your round requirements, you can **lay off** (add) individual cards to **any player's melds**:

### Laying Off on Sets
- Add cards of the same rank
- Example: Add 8♣ to an existing set of 8♠ 8♥ 8♦

### Laying Off on Runs
- Add cards that extend the run
- Example: Add 9♠ to an existing run of 5♠ 6♠ 7♠ 8♠

### Wildcard Replacement in Runs
When laying off on a run with wildcards, you can:
1. **Replace a wildcard** with the actual card it represents
2. **Move the freed wildcard** to the beginning or end to extend the run
3. Example: Run has 5♥ 2♦ 7♥ (2 represents 6♥)
   - You lay off 6♥, replacing the 2
   - Move the 2 to extend: 5♥ 6♥ 7♥ 2♦ (now represents 8♥)

## Ending a Round

A round ends when a player:
- **Discards their last card** AND has met the round requirements, OR
- **Lays off their last card** (if they've already met requirements)

### Scoring
- The winner scores **0 points**
- All other players count the point values of cards remaining in their hands
- Points are **cumulative** across all rounds

## Winning the Game

After 10 rounds:
- The player with the **lowest total score wins**
- Ties are possible (both players win)

## Strategy Tips

1. **Wildcards are powerful** - Use them wisely to complete difficult requirements
2. **Watch the discard pile** - Remember what cards other players are discarding
3. **Buying is costly** - Each buy gives you an extra card, making it harder to go out
4. **Complete requirements early** - This allows you to lay off and reduce your hand
5. **Count cards** - With 3 decks, multiple copies of each card exist
6. **Don't hold high cards** - Aces, 2s, and face cards cost you if someone else goes out
7. **Later rounds are harder** - Save your buys for when you really need them

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Creating more melds than required (you can only make the exact number needed)
- Trying to lay off before meeting requirements
- Forgetting that 2s are wildcards (worth 20 points if caught in hand)
- Attempting to take the discard pile after a buy was processed
- Creating runs that wrap around (K-A-2-3 is invalid)

## Interface Tips

### Managing Your Hand
- **Click cards** to select them for melding or discarding
- **Drag and drop** to reorder your hand (helps organize by suit or rank)
- Selected cards are highlighted and raised up

### Creating Melds
1. Select the cards you want to meld (click multiple cards)
2. Click "Create Set" or "Create Run"
3. If invalid, you'll get an error message
4. Use "Cancel Melding" to return all melded cards to your hand

### Laying Off
1. Select exactly ONE card from your hand
2. Click "Layoff Card"
3. Click on the meld where you want to place the card
4. If the meld is a run with wildcards, you'll be prompted to replace or add normally

## Game Controls Reference

| Button | When Available | Action |
|--------|----------------|---------|
| **Draw** | Your turn, draw phase | Draw card from deck |
| **Take Discard** | Your turn, draw phase, no recent buy | Take top card from discard pile |
| **Request Buy** | Not your turn, draw phase, haven't maxed buys | Request to buy the discard card |
| **Cancel Buy** | You have an active buy request | Cancel your buy request |
| **Pass** | Buy requests pending | Allow someone else to buy |
| **Create Set** | Your turn, after drawing | Create a set meld |
| **Create Run** | Your turn, after drawing | Create a run meld |
| **Layoff Card** | Your turn, met requirements | Add card to any meld |
| **Cancel Melding** | Your turn, have melds not yet confirmed | Return melds to hand |
| **Discard Selected** | Your turn, after drawing | End turn by discarding |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I create more melds than required?**
A: No, you can only create the exact number of sets and runs specified for each round.

**Q: Can I lay off multiple cards at once?**
A: No, you lay off one card at a time.

**Q: What happens if I disconnect?**
A: Currently, disconnected players cannot reconnect. This feature is planned for future updates.

**Q: Can I rearrange my melds after creating them?**
A: No, but you can use "Cancel Melding" to return all cards to your hand and try again.

**Q: Do I have to meet requirements to go out?**
A: Yes, you can only win the round if you've met the round requirements and have no cards left.

**Q: Can I buy during someone's meld/discard phase?**
A: No, buy requests can only be made during the draw phase.

**Q: What if two people want to buy?**
A: The player closest to the current player (next in turn order) has priority.

**Q: Can wildcards be laid off?**
A: Yes, wildcards can be added to any meld like regular cards.
